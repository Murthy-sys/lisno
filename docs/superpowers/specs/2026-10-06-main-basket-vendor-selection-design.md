# Main Basket vendor selection and unnecessary expansion removal

## Goal

In Procurement → Projects → Main Basket, allow the buyer to select any or all currently eligible vendors classified for that Main Basket, including vendors whose earlier invitation is marked `consumed`. Let the buyer act on that selection without silently discarding submitted bids or sending duplicate links. Remove the two unnecessary expandable sections below the **Vendor responses / Compare bids** details and the separate **Prior BOQ revisions** expansion. The POP / Gypsum basket in the first screenshot, with two included Main Lines and two eligible vendors with consumed invitations, is a required regression case.

## Current behavior and evidence

- The vendor candidate endpoint matches a vendor's saved `mainBasketIds` or legacy `mainBasketId` against the stable basket ID, then applies activation, official KPI, and contact-email readiness. Its response can correctly say that two vendors are eligible. See `backend/src/services/procurement-basket-vendor-eligibility.service.ts`.
- `ProcurementBasketEnquiry.tsx` disables every vendor checkbox when `BasketBoqEditor.canEdit` is false. A sent enquiry makes `canEdit` false until the buyer opens the separate **Revise sent BOQ → Start BOQ revision** control. `basket.boqReady` and refresh states can also freeze the picker, even though they concern dispatch readiness rather than vendor eligibility. The screenshot's **Comparison** stage and `consumed` invitation labels are consistent with a sent enquiry after bids were submitted. The screenshot alone does not prove whether another readiness blocker is present.
- The current `dispatch` operation accepts only a draft enquiry with no BOQ revision. A resend is limited to failed, expired, or stalled initial invitations without a bid. A vendor with a bid must use the counteroffer path, which requires an explicit reason. Updating a sent enquiry creates a new BOQ round and supersedes live links. See `backend/src/services/procurement-basket-enquiry.service.ts` and `backend/src/domain/procurement-basket-tender.ts`.
- The comparison panel is rendered by `ProcurementBasketComparison.tsx`. It contains the heading and explanation, **Refresh bids**, average, ranked vendor table, and two expandable sections underneath: **Line totals, GST and quoted amounts** and **Bid and counteroffer history**. The user clarified during implementation that the bid details must stay and only the two expansions should be removed. The same component owns Counteroffer, Award, saved-award review/withdrawal, and work-order drawer entry points.
- Existing frontend tests cover first dispatch and an explicit sent-BOQ revision, but do not cover selecting all eligible vendors after bids were received or adding a newly eligible vendor to the same frozen revision. The tender implementation and its tests are currently untracked work in a dirty repository; preserve them and other unrelated changes.

## Scope and required behavior

1. **Basket-wide selection.** Show every eligible vendor for the stable Main Basket ID, regardless of how many included Main Lines the BOQ contains. Keep the checkboxes keyboard operable in `draft` and `sent` enquiries; a consumed invitation or BOQ readiness problem must not change vendor eligibility. Preserve selection across search and pagination, provide an accurate basket-wide **Select all eligible** action, and show the selected count. Reconcile selections when a vendor ceases to be eligible; do not silently retain an ineligible vendor for dispatch.
2. **Visible participation state.** Distinguish `not invited`, `delivery pending`, `invited`, `delivery failed/expired/stalled`, and `bid received` in each row. `consumed` means the vendor submitted a bid, not that the vendor became ineligible. Present the selected vendors' planned actions before submission; never label an unconfirmed email or manual WhatsApp share as delivered.
3. **Act on a sent BOQ without needless revision.** For a vendor never invited to the current frozen BOQ revision, send that same revision to the vendor. Keep earlier links and bids valid. For failed, expired, or stalled delivery without a bid, retry through the existing resend semantics. For a vendor with a submitted bid, request an updated bid through the existing counteroffer semantics and require an explicit reason of at least 10 characters. For an active sent or pending invitation, report **already invited** and do not create another link. A selection containing different states must show which vendors will receive a first invitation, retry, updated-bid request, or no new message. If only already-invited vendors are selected, explain that there is no message to send.
4. **One reliable batch action.** A buyer can submit multiple eligible vendors, including the two previously bidding vendors in the screenshot, from one selection. Use a backend-authoritative batch operation for a sent BOQ so mixed actions have one version check, idempotency key, eligibility recheck, mail preflight, and per-vendor result. The frontend must not infer eligibility or participation from stale cached data. An unavailable mailer causes no new invitation, token, audit, or email write. After a partially failed delivery, show each vendor's actual state and a retry path; never claim the entire batch was delivered.
5. **Scope and workflow guards.** A new bid invitation always contains every included approved Main Line in the frozen vendor-facing BOQ, with stable source IDs and the approved quantity, UOM, and quote. A current revision whose vendor-facing approved scope changed cannot accept additional invitations or counteroffers; show a direct action to start an explicit new BOQ round. Internal-only cost drift must follow the existing current-scope rule. Do not invite or request bids after award approval/issue or bypass project-scoped authorization, source reservation, version/CAS, token isolation, expiry, revocation, or email delivery safeguards.
6. **Clear action states.** Selection stays available while the system checks BOQ readiness, but sending is blocked with a specific line-level reason when any included Main Line is incomplete or needs activation. Put the actionable reason next to the send control, including the path to revise a stale vendor-facing BOQ. Do not present a disabled checkbox with only an unrelated `consumed` status label.
7. **Remove only the unnecessary expansions.** Remove the **Line totals, GST and quoted amounts** and **Bid and counteroffer history** expandable sections below the vendor bid details, plus the separate **Prior BOQ revisions** expansion. Preserve the on-page **Vendor responses / Compare bids** heading, score explanation, **Refresh bids**, average bid price, priority/ranking table, vendor unit rates, top-priority marker, and **Comparison** progress step.
8. **Preserve bid and award access.** Keep the existing comparison, current bid details, Counteroffer, Award, saved-award review/withdrawal, and work-order drawer flows reachable in the **Comparison** step. A buyer can request an updated bid from a selected prior bidder through the vendor selection flow described above. The backend's comparison, recommendation, eligibility, and nonrecommended-award reason checks remain authoritative. Keep historical records persisted and auditable, without the two on-page expansions.

## Recommendation and alternatives

**Recommended:** keep one frozen BOQ revision and add a batch invitation action that routes each selected vendor by current participation state. This preserves existing bids, supports new and returning vendors, and provides one authoritative result for the whole Main Basket selection.

The existing **Revise sent BOQ** path remains appropriate when vendor-facing BOQ scope changes. Using it merely to invite another vendor would supersede valid links and bids. A frontend-only checkbox change would still fail because the backend's first-dispatch operation rejects sent enquiries.

For the comparison area, keep the existing bid details and award controls in place. Remove the two expandable sections beneath the details and the separate prior-revisions expansion as the user clarified. No new chooser or stage change is required.

## Data, API, UX, and safety impact

- Add a project-scoped authenticated batch endpoint under the current enquiry/revision. Input includes the expected enquiry version, current BOQ revision ID or digest, idempotency key, selected stable vendor IDs, and an explicit updated-bid reason when a selected vendor has bid. The response reports each vendor's resulting action and delivery status. Reuse existing `initial`, `resend`, and `counteroffer` invitation kinds, generation rules, hashed tokens, and bid history; no bulk data migration is expected. Keep the route-operation registry, OpenAPI, frontend API types, and query invalidation in sync.
- Preflight mail before persistent reservation. Create new pending invitations within a transaction with source, eligibility, invitation-state, and CAS checks. Perform delivery with the established safe state transition. Idempotent replay must not send duplicates; concurrent overlapping selections must not create two live links or counteroffers for one vendor/revision. Return explicit conflict/reload states when the candidate, revision, award state, or eligibility changed.
- Treat the candidate list as discovery and the backend as the final authority. Search, pagination, select-all, and status labels use stable vendor IDs. Keep selected vendors visible when changing search pages and expose the action needed for prior bidders. Use accessible names, focus states, an announced result, and responsive layout for the two-line POP / Gypsum view.
- Remove only the three disclosure controls and their now-unused presentation code/CSS. Retain the Comparison stage, ranked vendor details, current-bid fetching/refetch on return and after invitation/bid/award mutations, and existing award flow. The award drawer must still disclose the selected vendor's frozen quote, GST, terms, and required approvals. Keep historical BOQ and bid records stored for audit; this request removes their on-page expansion.
- No live invitation, production data change, seed, migration execution, deployment, commit, or push is authorized by this specification. Stub outbound mail and WhatsApp in tests.

## Assumptions and risks

- A consumed invitation in the current revision represents a submitted bid. The updated-bid action uses the existing reasoned counteroffer flow rather than overwriting that bid.
- The user wants any eligible vendor selectable for this Main Basket, even after an earlier bid. Existing active invitations may be selected, but they produce an explained no-op rather than duplicate delivery.
- Batch delivery can succeed for some vendors and fail for others. The UI must report per-vendor truth and retain retryability. The operation must not turn a successful earlier bid into an untracked replacement.
- The worktree is already dirty and the affected tender/frontend files are untracked. Implementers must inspect their current contents and preserve unrelated work before editing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. In a two-line POP / Gypsum Main Basket after both vendors have submitted bids, the two eligible vendors with consumed invitations have enabled checkboxes. Selecting both allows a reasoned updated-bid request; their current bids remain available for work-order selection until replaced by a newer response, and prior bid records remain immutable.
2. A new eligible vendor can be added to the same sent BOQ revision without changing its digest or invalidating other vendors' links, bids, ranking, or history. The BOQ includes both approved lines. Selection works across search and pages, and basket-wide select-all covers the reported eligible set.
3. A mixed selection correctly handles never-invited, failed/expired/stalled, already-active, and prior-bid vendors. No duplicate active link or bid is created. The UI states the action and actual delivery result for each vendor.
4. Ineligible vendors remain unavailable for dispatch. Backend revalidation rejects stale eligibility, source, revision, project permission, and award state. Missing mail creates no invitation/token/audit/email write; retries and concurrent requests are idempotent.
5. Missing readiness on either included Main Line blocks delivery with that line's reason while eligible vendor checkboxes remain usable. Approved vendor-facing source drift requires an explicit new BOQ round; internal-only cost drift follows the existing rule.
6. Focused backend domain/route/replica-set and rendered frontend tests cover the screenshot state, a two-line BOQ, mixed statuses, failures, races, search/pagination/select-all, keyboard and mobile interaction. Run affected typechecks/builds and repository hygiene checks after implementation.
7. The second screenshot's **Line totals, GST and quoted amounts** and **Bid and counteroffer history** expansions, and the separately pictured **Prior BOQ revisions** expansion, are absent on desktop and mobile. The **Comparison** step, vendor rankings/rates/prices, refresh, and bid details remain visible. A buyer with a current bid can complete award/approval/issue; saved award review and withdrawal remain reachable. Backend ranking, nonrecommended-reason enforcement, and immutable bid/audit history still operate.

## Open decisions

None required before planning. The recommendation follows the current frozen-BOQ, counteroffer, and immutable-bid contracts. Feedback on whether a previously bidding vendor should instead enter a wholly new BOQ round would materially change this specification.
