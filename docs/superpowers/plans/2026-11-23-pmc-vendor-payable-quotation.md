# PMC margin and vendor payable implementation plan

Follow the [approved specification](../specs/2026-11-23-pmc-vendor-payable-quotation-design.md).

1. **Trace and settle the pricing contract.** The integration owner records dirty paths and target diffs. Trace one included PMC line through catalogue, quantity and tier edits, save/reopen, quotation, publication snapshot, both customer PDF rendering paths and approved-estimate consumers. Record the current tax rounding boundary and distinguish unit rates, extended line amounts, tax and estimate round-off. Define the internal margin fields and customer-safe payable fields before assigning writers.
2. **Calculate and persist the payable.** The pricing owner reuses the authoritative PMC calculation with the configured maximum margin and the selected line’s current revision, tier, unit and quantity. Use its final vendor charges for the payable basis and its margin result for internal display. Keep calculations in integer paise, preserving quantity precision and existing tax and discount rules. Cover catalogue and draft contracts, server-side recalculation, quotation responses and snapshot creation. Preserve version checks and publication guards. Explicitly flag incomplete legacy drafts and read approved snapshots at their recorded values. Exclude internal margin fields from client-facing responses.
3. **Update estimation displays.** The frontend owner uses the agreed contract in Sales and Client rows and room, basket and estimate summaries. Show the maximum percentage and pre-tax Lisno amount on an included PMC Sales row. Reconcile displayed unit rate and extended payable after quantity, tier and mode changes and after reopening the draft. Verify PMC-off and mixed estimates. Keep incomplete amounts visible as incomplete.
4. **Render customer quotations.** The PDF owner updates customer quotation paths in estimate-pdf.service and estimate-proposal-pdf using the saved publication figures. Render tax-inclusive unit rates, including ₹/sqft where applicable, line amounts and one total payable. Update itemised pages, investment summary, payment stages and explanatory text consistently. Check that mixed quotations use a general payable label and neither separate GST rows nor internal margin appear. Keep previously issued documents intact.
5. **Reconcile and verify.** The integration owner tests the ₹100/sqft × 10 sqft example against ₹118/sqft and ₹1,180 inclusive. Use two unequal estimates, one mixed, and cover fractional quantities, low-quantity impact, supported discounts, rounding differences, missing data and tier/PMC changes. Compare saved line, room, basket, estimate, publication and PDF values. Exercise role-specific responses, save/reopen and publication on a replica set where applicable. Check approved baseline and downstream financial reconciliation. Render and inspect the PDFs, exercise Sales and Client screens at desktop and mobile widths, then run relevant suites, backend/frontend typechecks and builds, and git diff --check.

After step 1, pricing, frontend and PDF work may proceed in parallel on separate files against the settled contract. The pricing owner controls persistence and snapshot writes, the PDF owner controls rendering, and the integration owner resolves cross-layer changes before final verification. No production data rewrite is required by this plan.
